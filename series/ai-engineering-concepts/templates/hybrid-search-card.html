OVERVIEW

Hybrid search combines keyword matching and vector similarity in one retrieval step. The goal is better recall and more robust retrieval when exact terms matter and semantic similarity also matters.

TOOLS / FRAMEWORKS

  • ✓Vector layers: pgvector, Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant.
