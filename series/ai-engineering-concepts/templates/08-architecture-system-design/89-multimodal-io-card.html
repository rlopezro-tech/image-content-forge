MULTIMODAL I/O

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Multimodal I/O is the architecture pattern for AI systems that accept or return text, images, audio, video, files, actions, or structured data. It matters because each modality needs its own preprocessing, routing, validation, storage, and fallback behavior.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • ✓Set modality-specific limits for size, type, and duration.
  • ✓Design fallbacks when one modality is missing or weak.
  • ✓Validate outputs by format, safety, policy, and action risk.

PRACTICAL STACK

  • ✓Media intake: upload APIs, codecs, OCR, speech, parsers.
  • ✓Model/runtime: multimodal models, routers, tools, queues.
  • ✓Output/ops: validators, storage, traces, redaction, alerts.
