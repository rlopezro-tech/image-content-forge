MULTIMODAL I/O

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Multimodal I/O is the architecture pattern for AI systems that accept or return text, images, audio, video, files, actions, or structured data. Each modality needs its own preprocessing, routing, validation, storage, delivery, and fallback behavior.
