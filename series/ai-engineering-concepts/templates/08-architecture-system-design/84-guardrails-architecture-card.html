GUARDRAILS ARCHITECTURE

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Guardrails architecture places validation and policy checks around user input, retrieved context, tool calls, model outputs, and fallback paths. It matters because risk can enter at any stage, not only after the final response.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • ✓Validate inputs, context, tool calls, and outputs separately.
  • ✓Make policy decisions explicit, versioned, and traceable.
  • ✓Define fallback behavior for invalid or uncertain states.

PRACTICAL STACK

  • ✓Controls: schemas, policies, filters, allowlists, approvals.
  • ✓Runtime: gateway checks, validators, tool permissions.
  • ✓Ops: traces, audit logs, evals, alerts, red-team tests.
