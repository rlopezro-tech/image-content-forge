HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Human-in-the-loop architecture places review, approval, escalation, and audit trails inside the AI request path. Use it when decisions are risky, regulated, irreversible, customer-facing, or based on uncertain evidence that needs human judgment.
