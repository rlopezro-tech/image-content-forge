HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Human-in-the-loop architecture places review, approval, escalation, and audit trails around AI outputs or actions. It matters when decisions are risky, regulated, irreversible, customer-facing, or based on uncertain evidence that needs human judgment.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • ✓Trigger review by risk, confidence, policy, or cost.
  • ✓Give reviewers evidence, history, and allowed actions.
  • ✓Log decisions, reasons, reviewer IDs, and outcomes.

PRACTICAL STACK

  • ✓Workflow: queues, review UI, assignments, escalation.
  • ✓Controls: policies, approvals, permissions, rollback paths.
  • ✓Observability: audit logs, traces, reviewer metrics, evals.
