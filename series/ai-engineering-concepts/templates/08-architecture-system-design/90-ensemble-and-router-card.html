ENSEMBLE AND ROUTER

ARCHITECTURE PATTERN

OVERVIEW

Ensemble and router architecture chooses or combines models based on task type, capability, cost, latency, risk, and confidence. It matters when one model cannot optimize every request and the system needs explicit paths for specialists, fallbacks, and quality checks.
