AGENTIC LOOP

IMPLEMENTATION

OVERVIEW

An agentic loop is the runtime cycle where a model receives a task, plans the next action, calls a tool or model, observes the result, and decides whether to stop or continue. It matters because agent behavior is controlled by loop boundaries, state, tools, and validation.
