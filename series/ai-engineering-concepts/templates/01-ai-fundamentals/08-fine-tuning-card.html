Fine-Tuning

Core Concept

OVERVIEW

Fine-tuning teaches a model repeated patterns from examples. Use it when behavior, tone, format, or domain-specific decisions must be consistent beyond what prompting or retrieval can reliably provide.

HOW IT WORKS

1

Collect

Curate examples that show the exact behavior you want.

2

Label

Pair inputs with target outputs and edge-case corrections.

3

Train

Update model behavior on the example distribution.

4

Evaluate

Compare against baselines, prompts, and held-out cases.

5

Deploy

Version the model and monitor drift, cost, and regressions.

ENGINEERING NOTES

Best for behavior patterns, not fresh facts.
Still needs evals, guardrails, and rollback paths.
Bad examples create expensive, persistent mistakes.
