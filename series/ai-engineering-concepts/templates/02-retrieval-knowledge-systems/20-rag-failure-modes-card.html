RAG FAILURE MODES

PRODUCTION CHECKS

OVERVIEW

RAG fails when retrieval is weak, context is noisy, filters are wrong, or the sources are stale. The model can only ground answers in the evidence it receives.

HOW IT WORKS

1

Bad chunks

Documents split poorly
key evidence is missing
2

Low recall

Relevant content not retrieved
important source stays out
3

Wrong filters

Scope excludes the right data
or includes the wrong data
4

Noisy context

Weak matches dilute signal
model sees clutter
5

Broken answer

Unsupported response appears
confidence > evidence

PRODUCTION CHECKS

  • ✓Test retrieval before blaming generation.
  • ✓Track recall, grounding, and citation support.
  • ✓Fix chunking, filters, and freshness first.

TOOLS / FRAMEWORKS

  • ✓Observability: traces, sampled reviews, error analysis.
  • ✓Evals: RAG datasets, retrieval metrics, grounding checks.
  • ✓Frameworks: Ragas, DeepEval, Phoenix, Braintrust.
