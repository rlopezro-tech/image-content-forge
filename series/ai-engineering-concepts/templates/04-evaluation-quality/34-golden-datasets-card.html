GOLDEN DATASETS

OVERVIEW

A golden dataset is a versioned set of representative examples with expected behavior. It gives an AI system a stable quality baseline, so changes can be tested before they reach users.

ENGINEERING NOTES

  • ✓Favor representative examples over a large, random collection.
  • ✓Include failures that previously mattered in production.
  • ✓Review the dataset whenever product policy or behavior changes.

PRACTICAL STACK

  • ✓Data: sampled requests, curated fixtures, reviewed examples.
  • ✓Labels: expected outputs, rubrics, policies, annotations.
  • ✓Controls: versioning, ownership, CI runs, change history.
