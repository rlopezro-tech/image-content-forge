LLM-AS-A-JUDGE

EVAL PATTERN

OVERVIEW

LLM-as-a-judge uses a model and an explicit rubric to evaluate nuanced outputs at scale. It is useful for criteria that are hard to encode in rules—but only when judges are calibrated and monitored.
