AI system design is mostly tradeoff management.

A larger model may improve quality but increase latency and cost. More context may help recall but add noise. More autonomy may improve coverage but reduce predictability. More guardrails may improve safety but add complexity.

Strong systems make these choices explicit instead of hiding them inside one prompt.

Design reminders:
- Define the quality bar before optimizing.
- Separate deterministic workflow from model judgment.
- Route by task difficulty, risk, and cost.
- Add observability before problems become invisible.
