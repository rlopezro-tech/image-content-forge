A vector database is not just a place to put embeddings.

In production, it owns retrieval behavior: indexing, filtering, updates, tenant isolation, latency, backups, and operational visibility.

The right choice depends on workload shape. Some teams want vectors inside Postgres. Others need a dedicated vector system with managed scale, hybrid retrieval, distributed indexes, or advanced filtering.

Selection reminders:
- Check metadata filtering and permission needs early.
- Understand update and delete behavior.
- Plan re-embedding and re-indexing workflows.
- Evaluate retrieval quality, not only query speed.
