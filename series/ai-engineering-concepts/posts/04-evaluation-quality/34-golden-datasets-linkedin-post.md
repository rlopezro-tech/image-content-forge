# A golden dataset is the quality baseline your AI system can keep

Without a stable evaluation set, every prompt, retrieval, model, or policy change starts from intuition. A golden dataset gives teams representative examples and expected behavior they can run again and again.

The goal is not to collect every possible input. It is to preserve the examples that reveal whether the system is useful, safe, and aligned with the product.

Build it from real work:

- Include common tasks, edge cases, and high-risk requests.
- Record expected outputs, policies, or scoring rubrics.
- Version every change and document why a case belongs in the set.
- Add failures discovered in production after they are reviewed and sanitized.

A golden dataset turns past learning into a regression guardrail for future releases.
