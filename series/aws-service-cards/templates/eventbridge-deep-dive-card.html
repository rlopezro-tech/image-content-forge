Amazon EventBridge

Deep Dive

Key Takeaways

  • Event buses route many sources to targets.
  • Rules match event patterns and send events.
  • Pipes connect one source to one target.
  • Scheduler invokes recurring or one-time tasks.
  • Bus policies control cross-account access.

When To Use It

Decouple services with event-driven architecture.
Route AWS service or custom app events.
Connect streams or queues to targets with pipes.
Run scheduled tasks with Scheduler.

Watch Out

Events with no matching rules take no action.
Rules can invoke targets in parallel.
Cross-account buses need resource policies.
