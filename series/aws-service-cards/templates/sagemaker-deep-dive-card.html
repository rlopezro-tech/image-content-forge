Amazon SageMaker AI

Deep Dive

Key Takeaways

  • Covers the managed ML lifecycle: prepare, build, train, deploy, and monitor.
  • Training jobs run on managed infrastructure and produce model artifacts.
  • Inference supports real-time endpoints, async patterns, and batch transform.
  • Projects, pipelines, registries, and monitoring support repeatable MLOps.
  • IAM, VPC, encryption, logs, and audit trails are central controls.

When To Use It

Managed training and deployment for custom ML models.
One AWS workflow for notebooks, jobs, pipelines, and hosting.
Scalable real-time or batch prediction workloads.
Monitoring, governance, and repeatable MLOps.

Watch Out

Endpoint for low latency; batch transform for offline scoring.
Costs depend on instance types, runtime, storage, and traffic.
Model quality still depends on data, features, code, and evaluation.
