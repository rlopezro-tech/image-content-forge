Amazon CloudWatch

Overview

Service Focus

Observe applications, infrastructure, and AWS resources. CloudWatch collects metrics, logs, traces, alarms, dashboards, and events so teams can detect issues and respond.

What Is It?

Amazon CloudWatch monitors AWS resources and applications in real time, giving system-wide observability for performance, health, and utilization.

How It Works

1

Collect

AWS services, apps, agents, and OTLP send telemetry.

2

Store

Metrics use namespaces; logs use groups and streams.

3

Analyze

Dashboards, queries, and traces reveal signals.

4

Alarm

Thresholds or queries evaluate over periods.

5

Act

Notifications, automation, or events drive response.
