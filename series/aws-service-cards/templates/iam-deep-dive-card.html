AWS IAM

Deep Dive

Key Takeaways

  • IAM authorizes after a principal is authenticated.
  • Policies define permissions, often as JSON.
  • Explicit deny overrides allow.
  • Roles provide temporary credentials.
  • Access Analyzer validates and reviews access.

When To Use It

Grant controlled access to users and apps.
Delegate cross-account access with roles.
Replace long-term keys with temporary credentials.
Validate policies and reduce broad access.

Watch Out

New IAM users start with no permissions.
Do not use root for everyday admin tasks.
Trust policies and permission policies differ.
