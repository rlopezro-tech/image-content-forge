Amazon Bedrock

Overview

Service Focus

Managed foundation-model platform for generative AI apps. Build with model choice, inference APIs, agents, knowledge bases, guardrails, and evaluation.

What Is It?

Amazon Bedrock provides access to foundation models from Amazon and leading AI companies through a single managed service and API.

How It Works

1

Request

App sends prompts, messages, documents, or tool instructions.

2

Choose FM

Select a foundation model, profile, or custom model.

3

Add Context

Use knowledge bases, prompts, agents, flows, or guardrails.

4

Infer

Bedrock invokes the model and applies configured controls.

5

Respond

Return generated text, images, embeddings, or actions.
