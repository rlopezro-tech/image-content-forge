Amazon CloudWatch

Deep Dive

Key Takeaways

  • Metrics use namespace, name, and dimensions.
  • Logs are stored in log groups and streams.
  • Alarms evaluate data over configured periods.
  • Dashboards combine metrics and logs.
  • Alarm state changes can go to EventBridge.

When To Use It

Monitor AWS resources and apps in real time.
Centralize logs, metrics, traces, and alarms.
Alert on thresholds, queries, or SLOs.
Investigate health across accounts or Regions.

Watch Out

Metrics exist only in their source Region.
Each dimension combination is separate.
High-resolution alarms can raise cost.
