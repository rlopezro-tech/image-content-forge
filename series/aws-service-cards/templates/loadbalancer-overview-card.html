Elastic Load Balancing

Overview

Service Focus

Distribute traffic across healthy targets. Elastic Load Balancing routes incoming application or network traffic to targets such as instances, containers, IPs, and Lambda.

What Is It?

A managed service that receives client requests through listeners, evaluates rules, checks target health, and routes traffic to target groups.

How It Works

1

Client

Sends traffic to the load balancer DNS name.

2

Listener

Accepts requests on protocol and port.

3

Rules

Match host, path, headers, or conditions.

4

Group

Holds registered targets and health checks.

5

Targets

Receive traffic only when healthy.
