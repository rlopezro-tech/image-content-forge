Amazon Bedrock Guardrails

Deep Dive

Key Takeaways

  • Guardrails evaluate user inputs and model responses against configured policies.
  • Apply them during inference or independently with the ApplyGuardrail API.
  • Policies include content, topics, words, sensitive info, grounding, images, and automated reasoning checks.
  • Blocked input discards inference; unsafe output can be blocked or masked.
  • Account or organization enforcement can apply default safeguards across Bedrock calls.

When To Use It

You need consistent AI safety controls.
You handle sensitive information or PII.
You want prompt attack and harmful content filtering.
You need org-wide model invocation safeguards.

Watch Out

Guardrails complement app security and validation.
Policy choices affect latency, behavior, and cost.
Standard and Classic tiers differ by capability.
Enforcement requires ApplyGuardrail permissions.
