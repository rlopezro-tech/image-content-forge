AWS Series #34 - AWS Elastic Beanstalk

AWS Elastic Beanstalk is the AWS service for deploying and managing web applications without hand-building every infrastructure component.

You upload your application, choose a supported platform, and Elastic Beanstalk provisions the environment: EC2 instances, load balancing, Auto Scaling, health monitoring, deployments, logs, and configuration.

The key tradeoff: it gives you a managed platform experience while still creating normal AWS resources in your account.

Use it when you want fast application deployment with less infrastructure setup, but still need visibility and control over the underlying AWS environment.
